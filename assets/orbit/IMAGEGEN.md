# ORBIT background

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool in text-to-image mode. The output is used as the skin background. The HUD and controls are rendered by UltraMP3.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background only for an UltraMP3 Windows player skin called ORBIT. Create a portrait outer-space background with canvas aspect ratio exactly 176:208, straight-on, edge to edge. Deep near-black midnight-navy starfield with subtle indigo dust. The hero is a cool blue Saturn-like planet with finely textured clouds, a clean cyan-lit crescent and narrow tilted icy rings, placed toward the middle-right. Confine most of the planet and rings to normalized x=0.46–0.94 and y=0.32–0.63. A tiny dim crescent moon and sparse pinprick stars can add depth. Keep the upper 27% very dark and quiet, the left third from y=0.30–0.66 subdued for track information, and the entire lower 32% very dark and low-detail for the audio waveform and transport controls. Cinematic retro sci-fi, restrained and elegant, high-resolution astronomical illustration, navy and glacial cyan with a hint of deep violet. No text, no logos, no labels, no user interface, no typography, no foreground device, no border, no watermarks.
